## Related Work

Previous studies have examined the rise and decay times of solar flares. Swalwell et al. (2018) studied the durations of solar flares and discussed how their start, peak, and end times are determined. Bowen et al. (2013) also studied the relationship between rise and decay times and found that the decay phase was generally longer than the rise phase in their sample.

## Project Objective and Data Analysis

In this project, I want to compare the rise time and decay time of solar flares observed in 2023. I will use different statistical methods and tools to analyze the data and understand how the two times are related.

I have started analyzing the data using MATLAB. The box plots suggests that the median rise time was also longer than the median decay time. The bar graphs further illustrate the differences between the rise and decay times of solar flares.

I plan to use descriptive statistics, graphical comparisons, and statistical tests to examine the differences between rise and decay times. I also want to investigate whether these differences are statistically significant and compare my results with previous studies.
